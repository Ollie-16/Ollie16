16 GALLERY | FINE ART FROM UGANDA | PEARL OF AFRICA

True Fine Art Is Not Hanged.
It Commands the Room.

16 Gallery is an invitation to experience physical creation at its most authentic. Every canvas in our sanctuary is a 1 of 1 physical masterpiece, painted one on one by master African artists in the heart of the Pearl.

In a digital world overflowing with mass produced prints and AI generated duplicates, 16 Gallery preserves the rare weight of physical human art. Every stroke, impasto ridge, and shadow gradient represents hundreds of dedicated studio hours infused with African heritage, emotional depth, and uncompromising artistic soul.

From rich oil pigments matured over weeks to expressive acrylics and delicate charcoal lines, these works are crafted to serve as the crown centerpiece of private luxury residences, executive suites, and elite architectural spaces.

HAND PIGMENTED
